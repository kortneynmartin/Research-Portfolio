Project 01 • Food Safety

Peracetic Acid Decay Research

Extending the lifetime of peracetic acid antimicrobials in poultry processing

Project Focus

This project focused on prolonging PAA lifetime by identifying chemical stabilizers and developing synergistic mixtures, followed by confirming that these approaches retained antimicrobial effectiveness against Salmonella Typhimurium.

PAAS. TyphimuriumChemical DecayMicrobiology

Key Contributions

  • Identified chemical stabilizers and developed synergistic mixtures that could prolong PAA lifetime.
  • Conducted chemical decay studies to calculate PAA lifetimes.
  • Developed experimental design to test synergistic methods against S. Typhimurium.
  • Prepared PBS and nBPW media and performed culture growth and transfers.
  • Performed serial dilutions, bacterial plating, colony counts, and data analysis.
Project 02 • Analytical Research

PFAS Research

Improving method efficiency for PFAS analysis in poultry-related samples

Project Focus

This work focused on improving methodology for PFAS extraction, identification, and quantitation, while considering efficiency and consumable cost benefits.

PFASLC-MSSPEAcid DigestionLLE

Key Contributions

  • Completed a literature review and developed experimental plans to evaluate method enhancements.
  • Prepared egg, soil, feed, and water samples for LC-MS analysis using methods from CDC, FDA, and Agilent.
  • Performed homogenization, acid digestion, liquid-liquid extraction, solid-phase extraction, and vacuum filtration.
  • Wrote the end-of-year final report and prepared 10+ presentation slides.
Project 03 • Environmental Technology

Magnetic Nanoparticle Technology

Reducing wastewater contaminants using magnetic nanoparticles

Project Focus

This research aimed to reduce wastewater contaminants while reducing chemical use and sludge production, with the additional goal of potentially reducing operating costs.

NanoparticlesRefluxCentrifugationConductivityDAF

Key Contributions

  • Produced 15+ batches of magnetic nanoparticles designed to bind contaminants.
  • Synthesized nanoparticles via reflux and purified them through washing and centrifugation cycles.
  • Tested conductivity and calculated and adjusted nanoparticle concentrations for the DAF system.
  • Participated in pilot testing at the Woodstock Wastewater Treatment Plant, treating approximately 300 gallons of wastewater each day.
